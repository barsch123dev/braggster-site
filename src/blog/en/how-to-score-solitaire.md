# Solitaire Scoring: Klondike, Vegas and the Draw Three Rule

Solitaire, or Patience, is the card game most people have played the most times and understood the
least. Almost nobody who plays it daily could say what their score means, because most versions
either hide the scoring or invent their own.

Here is what the two real scoring systems actually are, what the draw rule changes, and how Braggster
scores a deal.

## The layout, briefly

Klondike deals 28 cards into seven tableau piles of ascending size, the top card of each face up. The
remaining 24 cards form the stock. Four foundations get built up by suit from ace to king, the
tableau gets built down in alternating colours, and only a king can move into an empty column.

Win the deal by getting all 52 cards onto the foundations.

## Standard scoring

The classic system awards points for progress:

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Card moved to a foundation | 10 |
| Card moved from the stock to the tableau | 5 |
| Tableau card turned face up | 5 |
| Card taken back off a foundation | -15 |
| Passing through the stock again (draw three) | -20 after the third pass |

Under this system a completed game scores well above 500, and the number rewards efficiency: the
fewer times you shuffle cards between foundations and tableau, the higher you finish.

## Vegas scoring

Vegas scoring counts differently. You are scored purely on **cards reaching the foundations**, and
the number is small: one point per card, so a perfect deal is 52.

The two systems answer different questions. Standard asks "how well did you play this deal". Vegas
asks "how much of the deal did you actually clear". Vegas is the one worth tracking across many games,
because it is comparable between deals in a way a progress score is not.

Braggster offers both.

## Draw one versus draw three

This is the rule that decides how hard Solitaire is, and it is not close.

**Klondike draw one** turns one card at a time and allows unlimited passes through the stock. Roughly
speaking, most deals are winnable, and losing usually means you played it wrong.

**Klondike draw three** turns three at a time, so you only ever see every third card unless the pile
length shifts, and Braggster's version allows three passes. The win rate drops sharply. A deal you
lose at draw three is frequently a deal that was never winnable.

If you are comparing your record against somebody else's, the draw rule matters more than the scoring
system does.

## Playing Klondike in the app

Braggster's Solitaire has a full tap to move Klondike board. Tap a card, tap where it goes, and the
finished deal scores through the same round path as a hand entered result. Cards on the foundations
are what get recorded.

The board follows whichever variant your match is set to:

| Variant | Board |
|---|---|
| Klondike, 1 card | Full board, draw one, unlimited passes |
| Klondike, 3 cards | Full board, draw three, three passes |
| Spider | Scored by hand |
| FreeCell | Scored by hand |
| Pyramid | Scored by hand |

The three variants without a board of their own are still scored: you play them with real cards, or
elsewhere, and record the result. They are on the list because people do play them, not as
placeholders.

Like Blackjack and the Freestyle table, the dealt cards are ephemeral. Only the score persists, which
is the right trade for a game you play twenty times an evening.

## Solitaire as a tracked game

The unusual thing about putting Solitaire in a score keeping app is that it makes your solo play
comparable over time. Most Solitaire apps show you a streak and forget everything else. Here the deal
lands in the same statistics as every other game, so "how am I doing at Solitaire" has an answer with
a number in it.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a good Solitaire score?**
Under Vegas scoring, 52 is a perfect deal and anything above about 30 is a strong partial clear.
Under standard scoring a completed deal typically finishes well above 500.

**Is draw three harder than draw one?**
Considerably. Draw one with unlimited passes lets you see every card in the stock. Draw three
restricts which cards you can reach, and Braggster caps the passes at three.

**Can I play Solitaire offline?**
Yes. The whole board runs on the device with no connection and no account.

**Does the app have Spider and FreeCell boards?**
Not yet. Those variants are scored by hand. The playable board covers Klondike draw one and draw
three.

**Are there ads between deals?**
No. There are no ads anywhere in Braggster.
